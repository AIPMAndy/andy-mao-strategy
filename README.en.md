### 🔴 Technical Strategy Decisions
Tech direction, investment decisions, market timing
- Spark Assessment → "Is it viable?"
- Contradiction Transformation → Find inflection points
- Three Stages of Protracted War → Judge phase
- Rural Surrounds Urban → Edge breakthrough

### 🟠 Team Management
Build self-organizing, high-efficiency teams
- Mass Line → From the masses
- Rectification Diagnosis → Identify formalism/factionalism
- Identify Main Contradiction → Find the "bull's nose"
- Investigation & Research → Go deep

### 🟡 Underdog Competition Strategy
Asymmetric strategy against stronger opponents
- Rural Surrounds Urban → Don't fight the strongest, occupy the weakest first
- Concentrate Forces → Win in one battlefield
- Asymmetric Competition → Avoid strength, attack weakness
- Contradiction Analysis → Find giant's innovator's dilemma

### 🔵 Long-term Strategic Planning
3-10 year roadmap planning
- Three Stages of Protracted War → Defense → Stalemate → Counter-offensive
- Strategic Protraction, Tactical Speed → Strategic patience + tactical velocity
- Contradiction Transformation → Quantitative-qualitative transition points
- Spark Assessment → Long-term viability

### 🟢 Alliance Ecosystem Building
Build ecosystem, drive industry evolution
- United Front → Expand camp
- Class Analysis → Judgment of interest relationships
- Unite and Struggle → Maintain independence
- Principled, Advantageous, Restrained → Balance

### ⚫ Crisis Response
Rational decisions under uncertainty
- Seek Truth from Facts → Check disconnection from reality
- Investigation & Research → Understand truth
- Identify Main Contradiction → Find core issue
- Contradiction Transformation → Judge qualitative change point

## 🎯 Data Integrity Principles

### Absolutely Forbidden

| ❌ Wrong Expression | ✅ Correct Expression |
|-------------------|---------------------|
| "We will win" | "Based on XX data, under XX conditions, confidence XX%" |
| "Market outlook is broad" | "TAM XX billion, 3-year capture XX%, confidence XX%" |
| "This direction will definitely take off" | "Based on XX indicator, at XX stage, qualitative change expected XX" |

### Core Principles
- Don't beautify competitive situation
- Don't blur judgments
- Don't confuse correlation with causation
- When uncertain, say "confidence XX%"
